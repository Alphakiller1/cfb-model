# P4-vs-G5 tier term — 2026-10-02

## Why

The 2026 shadow ledger's widest model-vs-market gap was in Power-4 vs
Group-of-5 games: model MAE 16.29 against DraftKings' 11.21 (n=70), with the
model under the book on the P4 side by ~10 points. The baseline report had named
the cause (sparse cross-tier scheduling leaves the P4 and G5 halves of the rating
solve weakly connected) and listed a tier term as the top open item.

## Method

`scripts/audit_tiers.py` rebuilds every 2021–2025 week on the production forecast
path — ratings, opponent-adjusted form, venue home field, consensus market — from
games strictly before the week (3,702 FBS-vs-FBS games, 29 CFBD calls a season,
cached thereafter). Candidate corrections of the residual were fitted on four
seasons and scored on the fifth, separately for weeks 1–4 and weeks 5+.

## Results (held-out MAE)

| candidate | weeks 1–4 (n=1,015) | weeks 5+ (n=2,687) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| current | 12.8367 | 12.3677 |
| **+ tier shift** | **12.6942** | **12.3669** |
| + intercept | 12.8451 | 12.3688 |
| + intercept + tier | 12.6937 | 12.3682 |
| + scale + intercept + tier | 12.7257 | 12.3739 |
| + home-field + tier | 12.6955 | 12.3669 |
| market | 12.0770 | 12.0809 |

On cross-tier games alone: weeks 1–4 MAE **13.02 → 12.67** (market 11.93, n=414);
weeks 5+ 12.22 → 12.19 (n=89). The shift toward the P4 side is +3.84 (fold SD
0.21) early and +3.64 (fold SD 0.79) later, and holds week by week in 1–4
(+4.4, +3.4, +3.9, +3.5).

## Decision

Adopted the tier shift alone (`tiers.SHIFT_POINTS`), applied after calibration so
the scale corrections fitted on all games are unchanged. Intercept, home-field and
rescaling corrections did not lower held-out error and were rejected. It closes
about a third of the early-season cross-tier gap to the market; the rest is the
information gap documented in `BASELINE_2019_2025.md`.
