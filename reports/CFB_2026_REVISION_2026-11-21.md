# CFB season audit and player revision — November 21, 2026

The core matrix remains unchanged because the tested replacements failed later-week validation. The deployed revision changes player opportunity allocation, injury propagation, uncertainty and the ability to review saved grades.

## Current-season matrix evidence

The replay covers 414 FBS-versus-FBS games in 2026 weeks 5–11. Weeks 5–7 (173 games) form the training window; weeks 8–11 (241) form the later validation window. Features are assembled strictly before each week with the production rating/form/venue path. These are retrospective input vintages, not a reconstruction of every original provider revision.

The fixed ridge matrix reweight raised validation margin MAE from 12.9366 to 13.0930 points. Margin rescaling raised it to 12.9458. Total bias correction raised total MAE from 13.6782 to 13.7820. None met the declared accuracy, squared-error and split-stability acceptance rules. No coefficient change was published as an improvement.

## Player revision

Conditional player shares are normalized when they exceed the team opportunity pool. Missing players' work is redistributed within position while preserving each recipient's own yards-per-opportunity rate. The previous 1.5x cap could leave substantial known backfield work unassigned. No known replacement means an explicit unallocated reserve; a missing backfield does not become quarterback carries.

Out and doubtful players are excluded. First-half absences split quarterback exposure and opportunity into halves; receivers follow the resulting quarterback environment. Questionable players carry an explicit available-if-playing assumption, not an invented probability of playing. Missing conference coverage is unknown. Returning players without a recent stat line carry a role warning.

Profiles expose historical role, normalization and availability changes, recent output versus the new mean, opportunity variability, team pass/rush script and shrunk opponent tendencies. The labels do not claim causal luck or coverage/route intelligence unsupported by the sources.

## Replay and ranges

The same pre-week environments compare the old and revised means. Historical ESPN closing benchmarks are supplemented by cached CFBD closing benchmarks when ESPN no longer supplies them. No final score is used as a forecast environment. Fitting uses 33,965 stat forecasts from 2024 and 35,076 from 2025. Another 28,650 stat forecasts from 2026 are excluded from parameter fitting and used for diagnostic validation.

On common 2026 forecasts, RB rushing-yard MAE fell from 30.655 to 30.295 and WR receiving-yard MAE from 26.697 to 26.399. QB passing means are unchanged by ordinary role normalization; availability scenarios are tested separately because historical timestamped reports are unavailable.

Yardage ranges are refitted on 2024–2025 outcomes. 2026 80% coverage changed from 74.5% to 83.7% for passing yards, 76.8% to 82.5% for rushing yards, and 75.6% to 87.1% for receiving yards; interval scores improved for those three. Counts use separate likelihood-fitted negative-binomial shapes and complete integer mass, including high-attempt tails and pushes. Count interval scores are mixed: attempts and completions widen and score worse on 2026. These distributions remain research outputs, not validated betting win probabilities. No global accuracy gain is claimed.

The parameter artifact is packaged with the model and publishes a canonical JSON SHA-256 and fitting seasons. The 2026 diagnostic data does not fit its parameters.

## Saved grades and automatic publication

The results page exposes saved game predictions, individual stat projections and original wager entries, with week, status, stat, version and search filters. Old forecasts and original lines are retained. Missing historical prices and closing quotes remain unavailable. Zero recorded production is distinguished from confirmed DNP/void status.

The collector grades native results alongside the existing complete weekly projection/CLV tracker every 30 minutes in the season window. It recovers the freshest published ledger and never creates historical forecasts. The full producer refreshes game-day availability hourly, using cached book snapshots on additional runs to protect the existing credit floor. Publication queues prevent the collector from displacing a full model build.

When CFBD's allowance is exhausted, a fresh ESPN scoreboard must verify every current FBS matchup before it can resolve schedule freshness. Optional CFBD consensus quotes may be replaced by the already required configured-book feed. Stale or failed efficiency inputs still block publication. The model uses strictly pre-week rating/form features consistently with its audit.

## Reproduction

Run `python scripts/audit_current_matrix.py --season 2026 --through-week 11 --out <research-folder> --key-file <existing-env-file>` for the matrix replay. Run `scripts/audit_player_revision.py` with an immutable baseline source path, output folder and existing key file for the player replay; `--reuse-replay` reruns fitting and diagnostics from the saved replay. NumPy/SciPy are research-only dependencies. Raw provider caches and replay rows are not included in the repository.

Machine-readable aggregate evidence: [CFB_2026_REVISION.json](CFB_2026_REVISION.json).
